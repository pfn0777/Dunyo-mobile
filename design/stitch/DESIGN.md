## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tier consumer electronics shopping experience tailored for the modern, mobile-first Telegram Mini App ecosystem in Uzbekistan. The aesthetic blends deep, obsidian-noir dark mode surfaces with radiant metallic champagne gold and neon cyan-mint accents, evoking high-end flagship tech hardware (titanium iPhones, precision-milled audio accessories, and luxury retail).

The design language leans into **Glassmorphic Precision Minimalism**:
- Silky semi-transparent dark sheets (`rgba(22, 24, 29, 0.72)`) with high-refraction frosted backdrops.
- Crisp hairline borders reflecting micro-reflections like diamond-cut chamfered edges.
- High visual hierarchy prioritizing trust signals: authentic warranty seals, quick installment badges (`0-0-12 muddatli to'lov`), and crisp localized price formats (`UZS / so'm`).
- Frictionless native mobile interaction designed specifically to fit comfortably inside Telegram's viewport constraints.

## Colors

The color palette reflects prestige, technological capability, and frictionless e-commerce trust:

- **Primary (`#E5B95C` - Imperial Gold):** Used for key identity items, primary conversion action states, VIP badges, ratings, and active tab highlights. Communicates luxury, flagship status, and exclusivity.
- **Secondary (`#00E5A3` - Neon Mint):** Used specifically for installment plans (`0-0-12`), monthly payment highlights (`so'm / oyiga`), discount tags, and in-stock badges. It draws instant positive financial attention.
- **Tertiary (`#3A82F6` - Tech Electric Blue):** Used for verified product badges, Apple/Samsung official ecosystem tags, and secondary action touchpoints.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - `Base / Canvas`: `#0C0D10` (pure obsidian black foundation).
  - `Surface Tier 1 (Card base)`: `#15171C` (deep graphite).
  - `Surface Tier 2 (Interactive glass)`: `rgba(28, 31, 38, 0.75)` with subtle gold/white specular rims.
  - `Surface Tier 3 (Floating sheets & overlays)`: `rgba(38, 42, 53, 0.85)`.
- **Text & Contrast:**
  - `Text High-Contrast`: `#F8FAFC` (pure readability against obsidian).
  - `Text Muted / Subtitle`: `#94A3B8` (cool metallic slate).
  - `Text Gold Accent`: `#F3D382` (refined accent copy).

## Typography

The type system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** with **Space Grotesk**:
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers warm modernism, exceptional geometric clarity, and high legibility across small mobile viewports for UI headers, product titles, descriptions, and catalog navigation.
- **Space Grotesk** serves as the technical financial voice, applied to numerical pricing, installment breakdowns (`so'm/oyiga`), memory capacities (`256 GB`, `1 TB`), model designations, and tabular specifications.
- Mobile scaling ensures zero horizontal clipping in compact devices (e.g. 360px width Android screens inside Telegram Webview).

## Layout & Spacing

This design system is optimized for a fluid single-column Telegram Mini App container:
- **Canvas Boundaries:** Screen padding maintains an invariant safe horizontal gutter of `16px` (`margin: 1rem`) on mobile, expanding to `24px` on tablet/desktop viewports.
- **Grid Structure:**
  - Phone (< 600px): Fluid 2-column card grid with `12px` (`gutter: 0.75rem`) horizontal and vertical separation.
  - Tablet / Wide Mini App (>= 600px): Responsive 3 or 4-column fluid layout with `16px` gutters.
- **Vertical Rhythm:**
  - Section headers to carousels: `space-md` (12px).
  - Inter-section spacing: `space-xl` (28px) to prevent cognitive overload.
  - Card internal padding: `space-md` (12px) compact padding for maximal product image visibility.
- **Telegram Native Safe Areas:** Persistent bottom margin reserve of `68px` plus `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` to accommodate the floating glass dock navigation and Telegram's native close/back action chrome.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphism anchored on deep obsidian:

1. **Level 0 (App Canvas):** Pure matte obsidian `#0C0D10` with faint, deep gold radial ambient gradients at header zones.
2. **Level 1 (Product Cards & Banner Containers):**
   - Background: `rgba(21, 23, 28, 0.85)`
   - Blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`
   - Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements, Search Bars, Modals):**
   - Background: `rgba(28, 31, 38, 0.92)`
   - Blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(229, 185, 92, 0.25)` (delicate gold reflection)
   - Shadow: `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 12px rgba(229, 185, 92, 0.08)`
4. **Level 3 (Fixed Bottom Navigation Dock):**
   - Floating pill design resting `12px` above device safe bottom.
   - Background: `rgba(18, 20, 26, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
   - Top highlight line: `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a refined, rounded geometric profile (`roundedness: 2`):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Chips, Mini Badges):** `8px` (`rounded-sm`) to `12px` (`rounded-md`).
- **Product Cards & Promo Banners:** `16px` (`rounded-lg`) matching modern smartphone body contours.
- **Action Buttons & Floating Docks:** Continuous full-pill radius (`9999px`) for touch-friendly ergonomics.
- **Installment Badges (`0-0-12`):** Micro pill shapes with tight `4px` corner radii for crisp data density.

- **Primary (CTA / Savatga / Xarid qilish):** High-gloss gradient gold fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F5D77F 0%, #D4A340 100%)`), text `#0C0D10`, font `Plus Jakarta Sans 700`, subtle warm aura glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(212, 163, 64, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary (Tafsilotlar / Outline):** Obsidian glass surface with a `1px` border of `rgba(229, 185, 92, 0.4)` and gold text.
